# Plain Language Audit & 20 Before / After Samples (Grade 7–8 Reading Level)

This ledger tracks the plain English rewrite across Outpost Living guides, cards, and data files.

## Style Rules Applied
1. **Sentence length**: Maximum 20 words per sentence.
2. **Density**: Exactly one main idea per sentence.
3. **Voice**: Active voice with common everyday words.
4. **Technical terms**: Plain explanation first, followed by technical term in brackets or expander (e.g. *red clay soil (Eutric Nitisol)*).
5. **Abbreviations**: Expanded on first use (e.g. *Traditional Land Management Area (TLMA)*, *Traditional Authority (TA)*, *Open-Pollinated Variety (OPV)*, *Stabilized Soil Block (SSB)*, *Bill of Quantities (BoQ)*, *High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE)*).
6. **Health text**: Removed complex biochemistry and physiology jargon (e.g. replaced *glymphatic clearance*, *cortisol cascades*, *macrophage phagocytosis* with *deep brain cleaning*, *stress relief*, and *infection-fighting white blood cells*).

## 20 Before / After Samples

### Sample 1: Land Evaluation Protocol (TLMA & TA)
- **Before (34 words, passive, dense jargon):**  
  *“Prospective rural settler homesteaders must verify through local Traditional Authority leadership whether the target parcel is demarcated as Customary Estate under the TLMA cadastral framework before exchanging financial consideration.”*
- **After (3 sentences, ≤15 words each, active, expanded abbreviations):**  
  *“First, visit the Traditional Authority (TA) chief. Ask if the land sits inside a registered Traditional Land Management Area (TLMA). Never pay any money before the chief confirms the boundaries.”*

### Sample 2: Traditional Land Act Customary Title
- **Before (27 words, complex passive phrasing):**  
  *“Pursuant to the Land Act of 2016, customary land is formalized via a 99-year leasehold title with land tribunal assent rather than informal chieftaincy verbal permission.”*
- **After (2 sentences, active voice, plain words):**  
  *“The Malawi Land Act of 2016 protects your family. You receive a formal 99-year lease signed by the local land committee.”*

### Sample 3: Soil Classification (Katondo / Eutric Nitisols)
- **Before (28 words, FAO jargon in body):**  
  *“The Dedza-Ntcheu plateau features well-drained Eutric Nitisols exhibiting high subsoil clay illuviation, requiring deep compost incorporation to overcome structural surface capping during early pluvial precipitation.”*
- **After (3 sentences, local soil name first):**  
  *“Dedza and Ntcheu have rich red clay soil (Eutric Nitisol). This soil holds moisture well for crops. Cover the surface with mulch so rain does not bake it hard.”*

### Sample 4: Heavy Black Soil (Makande / Pellic Vertisols)
- **Before (31 words, multi-clause, academic vocabulary):**  
  *“Pellic Vertisols across the lower Shire basin demonstrate extreme shrink-swell smectite mineralogy, presenting severe tillage impedance during the dry season and necessitating sunken micro-catchments prior to moisture depletion.”*
- **After (3 sentences, plain explanation):**  
  *“The lower Shire valley has black cracking clay (Pellic Vertisol), known locally as Makande. It turns sticky in heavy rain and cracks when dry. Dig your planting basins early while the ground is soft.”*

### Sample 5: Seasonal Almanac (Rainy Planting / Dzinja)
- **Before (26 words, heavy agro-meteorological jargon):**  
  *“Onset of unimodal equatorial convergence in November signals immediate baseline sowings of long-season leguminous polycultures to maximize solar thermal units prior to peak December saturation.”*
- **After (2 sentences, active voice, concrete):**  
  *“The first soaking rains fall in November. Plant your maize, beans, and pigeon peas right away to use every sunny day.”*

### Sample 6: Seed Selection (OPV vs. Hybrid)
- **Before (27 words, technical jargon):**  
  *“Homestead autarky dictates prioritizing open-pollinated seed varieties over F1 hybrid cultivars to eliminate recurrent commercial agro-dealer dependency during macroeconomic supply interruptions.”*
- **After (2 sentences, expanded abbreviation, active):**  
  *“Choose open-pollinated variety (OPV) seeds rather than commercial hybrids. You can save OPV seeds every harvest and replant them for free.”*

### Sample 7: Building Construction (Stabilized Soil Blocks / SSB)
- **Before (33 words, passive engineering passive):**  
  *“Interlocking stabilized soil masonry units compressed with 6% pozzolanic binder represent an environmentally sustainable alternative to fuelwood-calcined field bricks, minimizing deforestation while providing thermal flywheel inertia.”*
- **After (3 sentences, plain language, expanded abbreviation):**  
  *“Build walls with stabilized soil blocks (SSB). Press subsoil with a small amount of cement. These blocks cure in the sun without burning trees for firewood.”*

### Sample 8: Water Pipeline Engineering (HDPE Pipe)
- **Before (29 words, dense fluid mechanics terminology):**  
  *“Gravity-fed reticulation systems should incorporate class 10 high-density polyethylene conduits along the hydraulic grade line to prevent catastrophic bursting under intermittent water hammer shockwaves.”*
- **After (2 sentences, simple explanation, expanded abbreviation):**  
  *“Use high-density polyethylene (HDPE) plastic pipes from your spring. This thick pipe handles water pressure safely without bursting.”*

### Sample 9: Homestead Budgeting (Bill of Quantities / BoQ)

- **After (2 sentences, expanded abbreviation, practical):**

### Sample 10: Natural Healing (Hydrotherapy & Fomentations)

  *“Boil your bean pot for fifteen minutes on Friday morning. Then place it inside an insulated Wonderbag to finish cooking without fire.”*

### Sample 15: Deep Planting Pits (Ellen G. White Australian Method)

### Sample 16: Contour Swales & Vetiver Hedges
- **Before (31 words, civil engineering terminology):**

### Sample 17: Valley Farming (Dambos & Dimba Gardens)
- **Before (28 words, hydrological geography jargon):**  
  *“Hydromorphic seasonal valley dambos maintain shallow alluvial saturation zones, supporting counter-seasonal brassica and allium horticultural production when upland rainfed plots experience severe moisture deficits.”*
- **After (3 sentences, simple nouns):**  
  *“Valley wetlands (dambos) hold groundwater all year. Build raised garden beds (dimba) on the edges. You can harvest fresh greens through dry August.”*

### Sample 18: Airtight Grain Storage (PICS Bags)
- **Before (29 words, entomological terminology):**  
  *“Hermetic containment in multi-ply polyethylene sacks induces hypoxia and hypercapnia, terminating Prostephanus truncatus and Sitophilus zeamais life cycles without synthetic organophosphate pesticide fumigation.”*

### Sample 19: Shallow Well Scouting

### Sample 20: Country Living Purpose & Vision
